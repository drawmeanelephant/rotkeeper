## Start with what you have

A folded sheet, an old notebook, or a plain text file will do. Record the
date, the place, and one thing you noticed. A useful entry can be a sentence
long; it does not need to sound like a report.

- **Return to a place.** Repeated visits reveal the changes.
- **Describe before naming.** Shape, color, sound, and movement are enough.
- **Leave room for uncertainty.** A question is a perfectly good note.

## A tiny template

```text
Place:
Date and weather:
What I noticed:
What I would like to look for next time:
```

If you share a record, be thoughtful about sensitive places and the people
who live nearby. Not every observation needs a precise location.
