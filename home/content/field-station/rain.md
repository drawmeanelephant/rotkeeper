## A different map

After a night of rain, the path has a new geography. A shallow hollow holds
the sky. The dark soil at its edge is soft enough to keep a brief record of
passing feet. A leaf, caught against a stone, makes a tiny island.

By afternoon, most of it will be gone. That is part of the pleasure of a
notebook: it can hold a moment without needing to keep the moment still.

## The morning record

| Place | First thing noticed | Later change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Path edge | A line of small puddles | The edges dried first |
| Stone wall | Moss a deeper green | Pale again by afternoon |
| Garden gate | One curled leaf | Carried away by the wind |
