## A place to practice looking

The Field Station is fictional. Its notes are an invitation to slow down and
pay attention, rather than a guide to identifying plants or wildlife.

This little site also demonstrates the **Fieldnotes** theme: a local
stylesheet, readable long-form text, a few connected pages, and no JavaScript
or external font service. The [notebook guide](notebook.html) includes lists
and code; [After the rain](rain.html) includes a table.

## Make it yours

Choose `theme-fieldnotes.html` in a page's frontmatter, or select it as the
site default in the theme registry. The sample sources live in
`home/content/field-station/`; edit them like any other content, or use the
theme with entirely different pages.

For the template contract and accessibility checks, see the
[theme guide](../docs/themes.html).
