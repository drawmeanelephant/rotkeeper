## A little closer to the ground

The Field Station is a fictional notebook for the ordinary and easily missed:
the plant growing through a crack, the path that changes after rain, the bird
that always arrives just before the light goes. It is also a small sample
site for the Fieldnotes theme.

These pages are meant to be read slowly. There is no live feed and no perfect
inventory, just a few observations gathered in one place.
