## A border, not a boundary

At the end of the street, the pavement gives way to a narrow strip of gravel.
It is easy to pass without looking. Take a moment and the strip becomes a
little meeting place: grass leaning into the road, fallen leaves collecting
against the curb, a determined plant making a home in the smallest crack.

The most interesting places are often edges. They let different things meet,
and they change more quickly than the tidy spaces on either side.

> Walk the same route twice. On the second walk, look at the ground.

## Three things to notice

1. Where water has made its own path.
2. Which plants are growing in shade and which are reaching for light.
3. The places where a straight line has become a little less straight.
