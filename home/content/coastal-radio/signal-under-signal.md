# A Signal Under the Signal

The spectrum display looks like a comb. Jonas narrows the filter until the bright teeth become one thin thread beneath our broadcast. It is not music, speech, or Morse.

He says the signal is coming from land. I point to the bearing in the [listener's letter](letters-dark.md). At 2:04 a.m., the [lighthouse keeper calls](lighthouse-call.md).
