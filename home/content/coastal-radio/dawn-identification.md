# Dawn Identification

At dawn we identify ourselves: WNRD, Breakwater County, 88.7 FM, serving the boats and the people who wait for them. The sea is ordinary again.

Mara files the [station log](station-log.md). Jonas labels the reel. I leave the [quiet room](quiet-room.md) open on the monitor, because the light theme makes the page look like morning even when the tower windows are black.
