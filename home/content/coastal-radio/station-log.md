# Station Log: Night Watch

The booth is three rooms above the sea wall. Mara runs the board from 10 p.m. to 2 a.m.; I take the graveyard shift after that. The playlist is mostly weather reports, old harbor interviews, and the soft click of the automation system changing carts.

The [weather window](weather-window.md) opens every hour. If the red beacon over the transmitter blinks twice, we check [Buoy 17](buoy-17.md) before we answer any caller.
