# Letters from the Dark

The envelope arrives without a stamp. Inside is a bedtime story about a gull, a locked door, and seven blue lights. The first letters of each sentence spell a bearing.

We plot it on the chart beside the tower. It points offshore, toward the [signal under the signal](signal-under-signal.md). We read the story at 1:17 a.m., and the carrier tone stops.
