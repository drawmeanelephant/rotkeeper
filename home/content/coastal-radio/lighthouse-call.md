# The Lighthouse Keeper Calls

Keeper Vale speaks slowly, as though someone is asleep beside the telephone. A light has been circling the lighthouse foundation since midnight. It never rises above the waterline.

We ask whether it could be a boat. Vale says boats have wakes. This has none. Before hanging up, she asks us to replay the [weather window](weather-window.md) and listen for the missing word.
