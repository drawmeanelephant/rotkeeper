# The Foghorn Interval

Every ninety seconds, the harbor horn sounds. Tonight it leaves a gap: two long calls, one short, then silence long enough for the room to feel enormous.

The pattern matches the [three tones from Buoy 17](buoy-17.md). We switch to the backup microphone and open the phone line. The first caller is a child asking whether we can hear the [letters from the dark](letters-dark.md).
