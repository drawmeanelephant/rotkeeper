# Static After the Storm

The storm arrives at 3:10 a.m. and leaves at 3:13. The antenna survives. The tape deck does not. Every recording now carries a second voice, too low to understand.

We put the damaged reel in a drawer and make coffee. On the beach, there are nine black stones arranged in a line from the sea wall to the station door. The [dawn identification](dawn-identification.md) goes out at 5:59.
